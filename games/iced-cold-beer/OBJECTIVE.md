
How to Play

• Controls: Players use two joysticks to independently raise and lower the left and right sides of a horizontal metal bar.
• Objective: Balance a rolling metal ball on the bar and carefully maneuver it up a vertical wooden playfield filled with holes.
• Targets: You must guide the ball into a specific, flashing red-lit target hole while avoiding unlit sink holes.
• Progression: Each successful round moves the target hole higher up the playfield and increases the difficulty. Dropping the ball into an unlit hole ends the game.
• Scoring: A countdown timer rewards bonus points the faster you sink the ball into the target.
