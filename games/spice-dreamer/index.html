Spice Dreamer

Ride the glowing spice trail as a giant sandworm across an endless desert. Roll the mouse wheel to steer up and down the trail, and pulse Space to match the road's speed: too slow and you're left behind, too fast and you overshoot. Dodge sand fences and wrecked harvesters, coast across breaks in the trail, and climb the weekly, daily and all-time leaderboards. A new course every Sunday.

Music: "Spice Dream" by nic and reuben. Inspired by Black Emperor by BumbleBear Games.
